Welcome to the new website of **Night Sky Guides Ireland**!

We are a network of trained local guides who share a passion for the night sky above Kerry. On this site you can:

- meet our guides and find one near you,
- learn more about the Kerry International Dark Sky Reserve,
- and keep up to date with news and upcoming sky events.

Clear skies — and see you under the stars!
