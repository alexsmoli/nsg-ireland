With the clocks going back, the nights in Kerry are long and dark again — perfect conditions for stargazing.

## What to look for

- **Orion** rises in the east during the evening and dominates the winter sky.
- **The Pleiades**, a beautiful star cluster, are easy to spot with the naked eye.
- **The Geminids**, one of the best meteor showers of the year, peak in mid-December.

## Stay warm

Bring more layers than you think you need, a hat, gloves and a warm drink. Your guide will take care of the rest.
